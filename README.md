### Episode #1

_The Realm of Cybernetics_

What is cybernetics and why is it relevant in today's society? Considering computers, hardware, software, artificial intelligence, and robotics, the realm of cybernetics is one that describes this complexity in a compact and easy-to-navigate manner.

* Announcement
* What is Cybernetics?
* Why is Cybernetics relevant?
* What does it mean to everyday life?
* What problems can we solve that convey the most meaning?
* The nature of the computer
* The nature of the robot
* The nature of hardware and software
* The composition of artificial intelligence
* The schism of artificial intelligence
* How do we resolve the schism?
* Exit

### Episode #2

_What is possible with Cybernetics?_

This episode can serve as the bridge between the conceptual opening of `The Last Cyberneticist` and a concrete embodied research series.

Recommended turn:

* Move from abstract cybernetics to physical systems you can diagnose, repair, and test
* Introduce the `Hero-1` as a practical cybernetic workspace rather than a nostalgia object
* Set up the idea that embodiment changes what intelligence, adaptation, and control look like
* Point forward to a documented restoration and experimentation series
