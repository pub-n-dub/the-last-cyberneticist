# Series Music Cheat Sheet

One-page keyboard sheet for `The Last Cyberneticist`.

Date: Thursday, August 6, 2026.

## Goal

Record 4 short reusable cues:

1. main intro
2. interlude sting
3. closing cue
4. `Hero-1` variation

Keep it spare, clear, and repeatable.

## Sound

Use:

- soft electric piano
- mellow synth pad
- restrained organ-like patch

Avoid:

- bright jingle sounds
- drums
- fast arpeggios
- heavy effects

## Key and mood

Key:

- `D minor`

Mood:

- reflective
- technical
- patient
- slightly austere

Base motif:

- `D - A - C - F - E - D`

## Cue 1: Main intro

Use:

- start of every episode

Length:

- `8-10 seconds`

Tempo:

- `68-76 BPM`

Play:

`LH: hold D2 + A2`

`RH: D4  A4  C5  F5  E5  D5`

Note:

- let the last `D` ring
- start speaking after `2-3 seconds`

## Cue 2: Interlude sting

Use:

- one section break

Length:

- `2-4 seconds`

Play:

`RH: C5  F5  E5  D5`

Option:

- play `C + F` together, then `E`, then `D`

## Cue 3: Closing cue

Use:

- end of every episode

Length:

- `6-12 seconds`

Tempo:

- slower than intro

Play:

`LH: hold D2 + A2`

`RH: F4  E4  D4  C4  D4`

Ending:

- let it fade naturally

## Cue 4: Hero-1 variation

Use:

- Hero-1 and `eami` episodes only

Length:

- `8-12 seconds`

Tempo:

- `72-80 BPM`

Play:

`LH: D2  D2  A2  D2`

`RH: D4  A4  C5  F5  E5  D5`

Note:

- keep the left hand pulsed, not march-like
- this is the slightly mechanical version

## Recording order

1. Main intro
2. Interlude sting
3. Closing cue
4. Hero-1 variation
5. Record each one `3 times`

Take types:

- neutral
- drier and plainer
- slower and more spacious

## Editing

- trim handling noise
- keep natural tails
- use gentle normalization
- keep music low under voice

## Fastest usable setup

If you want something usable tonight:

1. choose one soft electric piano patch
2. record all 4 cues once
3. use the normal intro for general episodes
4. use the `Hero-1` variation for the robot series
5. use the closing cue without speech over it
