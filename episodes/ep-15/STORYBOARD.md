# Episode 15 Storyboard Specification

## Format

`Logic of a Pure Mystery: The Tati Robot` is a 25-minute visual investigation.
It should feel like an archival case file being opened, tested, and left open
with greater precision—not like illustrated podcast audio.

Narrative point of view: a present-day investigator assembling a constrained
argument in front of the viewer.

Visual rule: facts, inferences, reconstructions, and gaps receive visibly
different treatments.

| On-screen label | Meaning | Visual treatment |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `DOCUMENTED` | Directly supported historical material | Clean off-white evidence card; source shown or cited. |
| `INFERENCE` | Best explanation drawn from multiple clues | Amber card; connecting lines remain visibly provisional. |
| `RECONSTRUCTION` | Dramatic or illustrative rendering | Deep-blue frame and persistent label. |
| `UNKNOWN` | A genuine gap in the record | Charcoal/empty card; no line is drawn through it. |

## Board template

Each board records: `ID`, `time`, `image/action`, `on-screen text`,
`narration`, `evidence status`, `source or asset`, `sound`, and `transition`.
The board must also identify whether the asset is archival, newly photographed,
diagrammatic, or reconstructed.

## Key sequences

### 1–6 — The object (0:00–2:30)

- **01:** Black field; relay click; title appears as a case-file stamp.
- **02:** Full-frame Tati image; `OBJECT OF INQUIRY` label.
- **03:** Detail plate: head, eyes, jaw, chassis; annotation only says what is
  visible.
- **04:** Provenance card showing exactly what is known about Paris and what is
  not yet sourced.
- **05:** Empty maker field: `BUILDER: UNKNOWN`.
- **06:** Evidence contract: resemblance is not authorship; inference is not
  proof.

### 7–14 — What the form suggests (2:30–6:00)

Use restrained crops, measurement/feature overlays where source material allows,
and an amber `INFERENCE` frame. The narration may discuss finish, presentation,
and behavioral intent but should not assert operating mechanism, date, or maker
without a source.

### 15–24 — Berkeley's published machine world (6:00–10:30)

Use facsimiles and citations to establish Berkeley, Simon, Squee, and the
Porter/Jensen/Vall division of labor. Board 24 resolves to a simple skill map:
mechanical construction → electrical integration → functional completion. The
map is a model of a documented team, not a credit list for the Tati robot.

### 25–33 — The offsite-work clue (10:30–14:30)

Center the documented Koff material. A map/diagram can show the relationship
between Berkeley's office and Koff's apartment only when its geographic details
are verified. Any workshop scene is a silhouette-based `RECONSTRUCTION`, never
an implied historical photograph.

### 34–47 — Suspect table (14:30–19:30)

Every candidate receives the same four cards: `documented connection`, `fit to
the object`, `missing evidence`, and `what would change the ranking`. Candidates
are: named Simon team; Koff plus others; Berkeley; unnamed adjacent workshop;
independent line. The visual conclusion is a ranked *explanatory fit*, not a
list of accused people.

### 48–54 — Negative space (19:30–22:30)

Let blank fields stay blank. Show the France-family line in a card marked
`NOT SUPPORTED`, rather than making a dramatic detective-board rejection. Place
the mystery object back against the incomplete evidence map.

### 55–62 — Coda and next test (22:30–25:00)

Move from the original object to transparent modern diagrams of possible
subsystems: chassis, power, actuation, sensing, and control. Label these a
`REENACTMENT TEST`, not a replica claim. End with the archive question and a
return to `BUILDER: UNKNOWN`—now framed by a more precise account of the world
that might have made the robot.

## Asset checklist

- Rights/provenance check for every robot photograph and archival document.
- High-resolution scans or permitted reproductions of all source fragments.
- Original stills of case-file materials, tools, paper, and electromechanical
  textures.
- A consistent icon/diagram system for person, document, location, connection,
  inference, and missing evidence.
- Map only where the location data is independently confirmed.
- Separate asset folders for `archival`, `original`, `diagram`, and
  `reconstruction` material.

## First deliverable

Produce boards 01–06 as a 2:30 animatic before committing to the full sequence.
It tests the title, evidence legend, pace, visual language, and the balance
between mystery and restraint.

Build its soundtrack from the corresponding [audioplay plan](AUDIOPLAY.md): Host
and Archivist performances, original archive Foley, and the Tati motif. This is
the proof that the visual case-file and audioplay grammars work together.
