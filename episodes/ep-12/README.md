## Current Best Framing

The strongest shape for this material is not a standard historical episode, but a `case-file episode`:

1. Introduce the robot as a mystery object.
2. Establish the known Berkeley circle: `Simon`, `Squee`, and the named assistants.
3. Show the strongest evidence for decentralized workshop labor, especially `Jack Koff`'s offsite work on `Squee`.
4. Lay out the suspect field: `Porter`, `Jensen`, `Vall`, `Koff`, Berkeley himself, or a Berkeley-adjacent unnamed workshop.
5. Eliminate weak lines such as the unsupported France-family theory.
6. End on the stronger conclusion: the unknown robot most likely came from a machine culture that exceeded the published official line.

## Current Best Judgment

The best current inference is:

`The Tati robot most likely emerged from a Berkeley-adjacent workshop culture, with Porter, Jensen, and Vall as the strongest named analogues for the necessary builder pattern, and Jack Koff as the strongest Squee-side bridge figure.`
