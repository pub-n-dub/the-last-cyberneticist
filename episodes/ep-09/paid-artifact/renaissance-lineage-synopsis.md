# A Route Into the Renaissance

The Renaissance was not created by the Medici alone, nor was it a sudden awakening after a uniformly dark Europe. It grew from the movement of people, manuscripts, languages, and techniques across the Mediterranean.

The selected line runs from Christopher Allen Tucker through Kevin Warwick, John Hugh Westcott, Colin Cherry, Norbert Wiener, and earlier teachers to Gregory Chioniades, Shams al-Dīn al-Bukhārī, and Nasīr al-Dīn al-Ṭūsī. It marks one route by which Persian astronomical learning entered Byzantine scholarship, before Byzantine books and teachers helped make Greek learning available in Italy.

*Acknowledgement: we owe Persian scholars and institutions gratitude not merely for preserving Greek learning, but for developing it and enabling its Byzantine return—work that helped make later European renewal possible.*
