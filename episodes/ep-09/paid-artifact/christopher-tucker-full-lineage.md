# Christopher Allen Tucker: Full Recorded Academic Lineage

This companion artifact expands the bounded Tucker genealogy note into the complete advisor tree reachable from the Mathematics Genealogy Project records retrieved on 10 September 2026.

- The PDF is a single, large-format, vector document for zoomable inspection.
- The SVG is the corresponding browser-friendly vector diagram.
- The TSV is a searchable index of the recorded people, institutions, and doctorate years where supplied.
- The DOT file is the Graphviz source used to render the diagram.

The diagram contains 307 unique recorded people and 372 recorded advisor relationships. It traces only advisor relationships exposed by the source records; it does not claim a complete intellectual, institutional, or personal history.

## Primary source

Mathematics Genealogy Project, “Christopher Allen Tucker,” ID 177516, accessed 10 September 2026:  
https://genealogy.math.ndsu.nodak.edu/id.php?id=177516
