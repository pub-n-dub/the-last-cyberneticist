## Perhaps the *first* "artificial intelligence" usage

In Edmond Hamilton's novelet *Moon of the Unforgotten*, published in the January 1951 issue of *Startling Stories*, the printed magazine page 120 uses the phrase "artificial intelligence" in a recognizably modern sense.

The seventh full paragraph describes Grag, a metal-bodied companion of Curt Newton, as having "the strength of an army and an artificial intelligence equal to the human."

The relevant scan is page 120 of `Startling Stories - January 1951` (page 3 of the extracted novelet PDF).

This is a notable perhaps the first modern use of the term for machine intelligence comparable to human intelligence that McCarthy lifted and Wiener later leveraged in terms of _Operations Research_.
