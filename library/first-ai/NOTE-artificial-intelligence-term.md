# Early "artificial intelligence" usage

In Edmond Hamilton's novelet *Moon of the Unforgotten*, published in the
January 1951 issue of *Startling Stories*, the printed magazine page 120 uses
the phrase "artificial intelligence" in a recognizably modern sense.

The seventh full paragraph describes Grag, a metal-bodied companion of Curt
Newton, as having "the strength of an army and an artificial intelligence equal
to the human."

The relevant scan is page 120 of `Startling Stories-January 1951.pdf` (page 3 of the extracted novelet PDF).
This is a notable early fictional use of the term for machine intelligence
comparable to human intelligence.
